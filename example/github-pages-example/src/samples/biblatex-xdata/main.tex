\section{Data Containers}

An \texttt{@xdata} entry holds fields that other entries inherit through their
\texttt{xdata} field, so a fixed publisher and location combination is written once
and reused \parencite{xdata:single}. Containers may cascade, one container pulling
in several others \parencite{xdata:cascade}, and single names or list items may be
referenced individually \parencite{xdata:granular}. Container entries are consumed
by Biber and never appear in the bibliography themselves.

\section{Ordering}

The \texttt{sample} template sorts on \texttt{presort}, then on a \texttt{sortkey}
that overrides every later directive, then on name, title and year. One entry is
filed under a substitute \texttt{sortname} instead of its author
\parencite{sorting:sortname} and another is pushed to the end of both lists by its
\texttt{sortkey} \parencite{sorting:sortkey}. Switching to the built-in
\texttt{ynt} template reorders the remaining entries by year, where the
\texttt{sortyear} of \textcite{xdata:cascade} takes precedence over its date.
